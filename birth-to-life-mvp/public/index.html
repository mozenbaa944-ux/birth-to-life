How Birth-to-Life Works

A seamless four-step journey that begins at birth and builds a lifelong intelligent health record.

01
🏥
Hospital Registers Birth

The partner hospital instantly creates a digital health record at the moment of birth, linked to the family account.

02
👨‍👩‍👧
Parents Get Full Access

Parents receive the mobile app with real-time records, vaccination schedules, growth milestones, and alerts.

03
👨‍⚕️
Doctors & Labs Add Data

Every consultation, prescription, and lab result is securely appended to the record by authorized providers.

04
📊
Lifelong Health Timeline

The record grows through every stage of life — enabling preventive, personalized healthcare from day one.
